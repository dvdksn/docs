A Docker organization is a collection of teams, members, and repositories with
centralized management. Companies group multiple organizations under a shared
set of administrators and policies.

Use the [Docker Admin Console](https://app.docker.com/admin) to create and
manage your company and its organizations, assign roles to members, and
control access across your environments.

For an overview of the company and organization hierarchy, see the
[Administration overview](/admin/).
